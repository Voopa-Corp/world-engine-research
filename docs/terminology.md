# Terminology

The vocabulary below names computational interfaces. It does not assert a one-to-one correspondence with biological structures or a complete representation of a person.

## World Engine

The complete framework for maintaining uncertain behavioral state, detecting Signals, applying Functions, evaluating candidate transitions, and representing relational feedback through World State.

## Signal Intelligence

Detection and validation of predictive patterns in an event field. A Signal can combine response order, relative behavior, temporal structure, collective dynamics, context, and change. A trending Signal is not necessarily a trending keyword, person, or content item.

## Archetype

A shared prior over platform-relevant transition dynamics. An Archetype can encode tendencies such as habituation speed, novelty response, deliberation, sensitivity to social evidence, or persistence of an active goal. It is not a demographic category, fixed personality type, or topic preference.

## Digital Mind

A reusable computational transition hypothesis. It contains a state representation, transition model, emission model, retrieval policy, Function configuration, and uncertainty model. One Archetype can generate many Digital Minds. One Digital Mind can support many synthetic identities, while a real user can retain posterior support across several Digital Minds.

## Identity

A real or synthetic actor associated with observations and posterior weights. An identity gives a Digital Mind situated expression through content, relationships, context, history, and social position. It is not intended to imitate a named person.

## Function

A reusable state-transition operator. Candidate Functions include attention allocation, memory retrieval, novelty response, value estimation, goal activation, social influence, inhibition, and context gating. The names describe computational roles rather than neural regions.

## World State

The evolving population of Digital Minds and identities, content and external entities, relationship graph, shared context, and deployed policy. It makes feedback and nonstationarity explicit.
